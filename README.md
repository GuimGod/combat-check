# Combat Check

An attention check for your own gear. With the weapon **you** chose to hold, it reminds you of these:

1. **The attack style.** Which melee style of the weapon in your hand deals the most damage to the monster you are fighting, when another one is selected.
2. **The switch pieces you left behind.** The pieces that go with the weapon in your hand and are still sitting in your inventory. A misclick during a seven-piece switch is easy to miss. After the count it writes what they still add ("4 left +18%"); the figure shrinks with each piece you put on.
3. **A better weapon left in your inventory.** When a weapon you are carrying, with the pieces that go with it, would deal more to the monster you are fighting than the weapon in your hand with its own pieces (by at least the "Weapon: minimum gain" setting, 1% by default; it takes steps of 0.1, so 0.5 is half a percent, and 0 shows any gain from +0.1%: under 10% the gain has one decimal, "+0.1%", "+1.1%"), it shows that weapon's icon and how much more ("+75%"), and can mark it in the inventory ("Mark them in the inventory", off by default). While an attack prayer is on, a weapon of another combat class is counted with the best attack prayer you have for its own class (with Rigour on, a sword in the inventory is compared with Piety), and so is the weapon in your hand, so a prayer left on from another style is not read as a better weapon. Never sooner than 3 seconds after that became true: it reminds you of a switch you left undone, it does not tell you what is coming. With a weapon in hand that is held for its special attack (dragon claws, a godsword, a dragon warhammer, the Voidwaker and the like), it does not point back at the other weapon: when to go back is your own timing. The same with a Venator bow in hand: its worth is in the arrow that bounces to other monsters, and the figures here are for one monster only; and with a burst or a barrage of the Ancient Magicks on autocast, which hit up to nine monsters (a rush and a blitz hit one and are compared as usual). The Tonalztics of Ralos is one of the weapons held for their special attack: it lowers the monster's defence.

4. **The attack prayer.** While you fight, it reminds you when no attack prayer is on, naming the best one you have for the weapon in your hand ("Piety off", "Eagle eye off"; without Piety or Chivalry, which need the Knight Waves Training Grounds, melee takes two prayers, so it says "Melee prayer off"; Rigour and Augury need Defence 70 besides their scroll, so below it a bow is counted with Deadeye and a staff with Mystic vigour), and when the one that is on belongs to another style ("Wrong prayer: Rigour", with a sword in hand). Each of the two has its own switch. The notice carries the prayer's icon, crossed out when it is the wrong one, and how much more the gear you are wearing would deal to that monster with the prayer it names ("Augury off +17%"; "Show the gain %" setting): the prayer's bonus counts for less where your accuracy is already near the top, so against a monster with a low defence for your style it can be a couple of percent, and the same prayer can be worth far more against another. It goes away about 5 seconds after you stop attacking, and is not shown with no prayer points left. It looks only at your own weapon and your own prayers: it has nothing to do with protection prayers or with what the monster does.

5. **A prayer left on.** Out of combat for about 10 seconds with a prayer still draining, it says "Prayer still on", with the icons of the prayers that are on. Preserve, Rapid Heal and Rapid Restore are left alone. Only an NPC you can attack counts as combat: a pet following you, a banker or a grave you clicked on does not hold the reminder back.

The figures come from the standard accuracy and max hit formulas for melee, ranged and staves with their own attack, checked against the OSRS Wiki calculator. Weapons whose strength depends on what the monster is are counted against that monster: demonbane weapons on demons (scaled for Duke Sucellus and Yama; the Arclight without charges as the Darklight, as the OSRS Wiki says, where the calculator keeps its 70%), the dragon hunter lance, crossbow and wand on dragons, keris partisans on kalphites (the Keris partisan of amascut with the stab and strength of the OSRS Wiki and the calculator, which RuneLite's item data lacks, and less of both outside the Tombs of Amascut), the leaf-bladed battleaxe, the revenant weapons inside the Wilderness with their ether in them, the Crystal bow and the Bow of Faerdhinen with the crystal armour of Prifddinas (not the Gauntlet's bow or armour, and not a piece without charges), the Colossal blade, the Tonalztics of Ralos (twice charged, once uncharged), the Dark bow's two arrows, the Comp ogre bow (the ammunition's strength alone), the TzHaar weapons with the obsidian armour and the Berserker necklace, Dinh's bulwark (and its blazing dress) in its Pummel style with the strength the defence bonuses worn add (the stab, slash, crush and ranged defence less 800, over 12, less 38: OSRS Wiki, Dinh's bulwark; its Block style does not attack), Ahrim's set with the Amulet of the damned and the Void sets (the Void mage set too). A weapon that cannot attack without its charges (Tumeken's shadow, the Sanguinesti staff, the Eye of Ayak and the Warped sceptre uncharged, Craw's bow and the Webweaver bow without ether, the Bow of Faerdhinen and the Crystal bow inactive) gets no figure and is never suggested; the Scythe of vitur and the Venator bow attack uncharged and are counted. The raids' monsters are counted as the raid scales them, with the party, mode, raid level and path levels read from the game (see "Chambers of Xeric", "Theatre of Blood" and "Tombs of Amascut"), and so are the other fights the calculator has a rule of its own for (see "Other fights with a rule of their own"). Ruby and diamond bolts (e) are counted with the monster's current health (the ruby effect takes a share of it, so it is worth less as the fight goes on): when the other of the two, in your inventory, would deal more now, it is shown in a box of its own, in green ("Diamond +3%"). With a crossbow in hand and no bolts on at all, the bolts to put on are shown the same way: the better of the two kinds against the monster you are fighting, or, with no monster yet, the ruby ones first, then the diamond ones, then any others. While the Bolt Swap Reminder plugin is on, it gives this reminder and this plugin leaves it out. When the better weapon in your inventory is a crossbow, it is compared with the best bolts you have on you, and those bolts are shown and marked with it, so the two are switched together; with no bolts at all, a crossbow is not suggested. Karil's crossbow is counted with bolt racks only. A bow or a crossbow counts only the ammunition it can fire, as the calculator lists it: no dragon arrows in a Magic shortbow (i), no dragon bolts in a Rune crossbow, the brutal arrows in a Comp ogre bow; a weapon with only ammunition it cannot fire is not suggested, and such bolts are not asked for. The Ancient Magicks on autocast are counted with their own max hits, on the one monster attacked (with a burst or a barrage in hand no other weapon is pointed at). A staff with an elemental spell of the standard spellbook on autocast is counted too: the spell's max hit at your Magic level, the monster's weakness to its element when it has one (from the OSRS Wiki's monster data), the charged tome of that element and the Harmonised nightmare staff's faster casting. No figure is shown, and no weapon is suggested, for what is still not modelled: the enchanted bolts whose effect deals damage of its own other than ruby and diamond (opal, pearl, dragonstone, onyx), a blowpipe whose darts have not been seen, a staff with a spell on autocast that is neither an elemental one of the standard spellbook nor one of the Ancient Magicks (Magic Dart and Iban Blast among them, and the Staff of the dead in melee: a staff always counts as magic), the Soulreaper axe, Verac's flail, chinchompas and a few others (and the three weapons of "Limits" that attack only somewhere else). The Gauntlet's staves are counted with their own max hit. Ammunition kept in Dizana's quiver is counted as the one being shot when the quiver is worn (or put on by the switch, from the inventory) and the ammunition slot holds none of that kind; with the quiver in the bank its ammunition is not counted. The bonus the quiver itself gives (charged, blessed or as Dizana's max cape: 10 more ranged accuracy and 1 more ranged strength on the arrows, bolts and javelins shot) is counted, as the calculator counts it.

## The Fractured Archive's weapons

RuneLite's item data does not have the raid's rewards yet, so their bonuses are the OSRS Wiki's (read 2026-10-22) until it does.

- **Obligator.** Its Thrust (5 ticks) rolls the damage twice, three times on a monster whose stab or slash defence is higher than its crush, four times when both are, and keeps the best roll; each roll is 20% more on a 2x2 monster and 40% more on a 3x3 or larger one; the monster's flat armour comes off the final hit once. Its Smash attacks every 3 ticks with one roll and no size bonus. The attack style notice compares the two per tick. One roll of accuracy is taken for all the damage rolls: the game's posts do not say, and if each roll had its own the Thrust would deal more. Uncharged it gets no figure (the wiki does not say whether it attacks).
- **Zeal.** 25% Attack and 28% Strength, in Piety's place. Once unlocked it is the melee prayer the reminder names ("Zeal off"), with its icon (sprite 8595, from RuneLite's game values of 2026-10-20).
- **Ascension crossbows.** 3 ticks, 2 on Rapid; they fire the Ascension bolts and nothing else, and no other crossbow fires those. The Diamond ascension bolts (e) are counted with the diamond's effect at half its chance; the onyx ones get no figure, like any onyx bolts.
- **Zorya's tome.** No figure: its max hit by the Magic level is not published. It is held for its special attack, so with it in hand no other weapon is pointed at.

## What is on

The first section of the settings has one switch for each reminder: the attack style ("Combat check"), switch pieces left behind, pieces to take off, better weapon in the inventory, better weapon in the bank, ruby or diamond bolts (and whether their difference is shown all the time or only once the other bolts deal more), no attack prayer on, wrong attack prayer on, prayer left on, and the request to Check a blowpipe. The Slayer task is always read: it has no switch. Everything else in the settings is how each one looks and how long it waits.

## Where it is drawn

Every notice is in one overlay, at the bottom right by default. Hold Alt and drag it to move it, like any RuneLite overlay. Only the outline of the best style's button stays where the button is.

## Language

The words the plugin writes on screen ("Wrong prayer", "2 left", "Prayer still on") can be shown in English, Portuguese, Spanish, German or French: the "Language" setting. Names of items, prayers and attack styles stay as the game has them, and the settings panel itself is in English.

## What it never does

- It never names what a monster is weak to. Its defences and elemental weakness, as the OSRS Wiki lists them, only go into the figure of what your own gear would do to it.
- It never anticipates anything a monster will do.
- The Toxic blowpipe (and the Blazing one), and the Camphor, Ironwood and Rosewood blowpipes, are counted with the darts inside them. The game only tells which darts those are in the message of their Check option, so the plugin reads that message and remembers the darts of each blowpipe for the account (a message with Zulrah's scales is the Toxic blowpipe's; one without is the one of the other three last checked: its exact words are not seen yet); until it has seen one, "Check your blowpipe" is shown while the blowpipe is in hand (it has its own switch), and the blowpipe gets no figure. Arrows, bolts or javelins left in the ammunition slot give a blowpipe or a thrown weapon nothing.
- Against a monster that changes form or phase during the fight it only points at a better weapon of the same combat class as the one in your hand (a better melee weapon while you hold a melee weapon), never at a change of class (the Nylocas, Zulrah, the Grotesque Guardians, the Nightmare, the Hunllef, the Phantom Muspah, Nex, the Fractured Archive's Archive Guardian, Ocellus and Bork with the Smorks, and others): which weapon each form calls for is the fight itself. Verzik Vitur is not one of them: each of her phases is plain to see and lasts, so on every phase, in Entry, normal and Hard Mode, any weapon you carry is pointed at, as against a boss that does not change form (on her second phase, Tumeken's shadow with the magic switch over the Scythe, when it deals more). On her first phase she charges a magic attack every 14 ticks, which leaves room for two attacks of any 4 or 5-tick weapon (OSRS Wiki, Theatre of Blood/Strategies), so melee weapons of 4 and 5 ticks are compared there by what one attack deals, each hit held at 10 by her shield; nothing is pointed at with ranged, magic or a weapon of another speed in hand, nor to them. The Dawnbringer has no figure: it is never pointed at, and nothing is while it is in your hand. Against Akkha in the Tombs of Amascut no weapon of the style he is praying against is pointed at, and in his enrage, where he prays against magic and missiles, only a melee one is. Against the Royal Titans no ranged weapon is compared: their ranged accuracy is six times while they stand out of melee distance, which the plugin cannot tell. Once you have changed weapon yourself, the pieces that go with it are listed as usual, and so is the best attack style.
- It does not click, equip, change the attack style or touch any menu. It only draws.
- It says nothing in a fight with another player. Where players can attack each other (the Wilderness, or wherever the game sets its PvP area varbit, as on a PvP world: what RuneLite's Player Indicators reads for its "PvP" setting), while you target a player or a player targets you, and for a minute after, every reminder is off: it is a tool for fighting monsters. Anywhere else a player targeting you, or targeted, is following or trading (the game calls all of these "interacting"): an alt or a friend following you keeps nothing off. In the Wilderness a player following you cannot be told from one attacking you, and silences it too.

## The delays

Four waits are yours to set, in game ticks (0.6 seconds each):

| Reminder | Setting | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Attack style | "Attack style (bosses)": how long the wrong style must stay selected, against a boss | 0 |
| Switch pieces | "Switch pieces": how long a piece must stay unworn; raise it if the reminder flashes during a normal switch | 0 |
| Attack prayer | "Attack prayer": how long it must stay wrong or off | 0 |
| Prayer left on | "Prayer left on": how long out of combat with a prayer on, and how long the prayer has been on | 17 (about 10 seconds) |

By default every reminder stays on screen for as long as what it reminds you of is true: it goes when you put it right, or when the fight it belongs to is over (the monster died, a minute passed without an attack, you walked away or attacked another). Each time can be lowered in the "Delays" section:

| Reminder | By default | To show it for a while instead |
|---|---|---|
| No attack prayer on | "Attack prayer: keep on screen" on: until an attack prayer is on | turn it off: shown for "Attack prayer: show for" (5 seconds), then quiet until you turn an attack prayer on (and fight without one again) |
| Pieces to put on or take off | "Switch pieces: keep on screen" on: until the switch is complete | turn it off: shown for "Switch pieces: show for" (5 seconds), then again when you change a piece of armour (a shield counts, the weapon and the ammunition do not), or a new fight begins: after a minute with no attack, or farther than 20 tiles from the last one (the next room of a raid). The next monster of the same room or task is not a new fight |
| The attack prayer reminder after your last attack | "Attack prayer: after the last attack" 0: while the monster is the one you fight | seconds after your last attack (it was 5) |
| The ruby or diamond bolts reminder after your last attack | "Bolts: after the last attack" 0: while the monster is the one you fight, at the last health it showed | seconds after your last attack (it was 15) |
| A monster of your Slayer task after it dies | "After a kill on task" 0: kept for the next one until a minute passes without an attack, you walk away or attack another | seconds after the kill (it was 10) |

With a time set, the pieces have one time for a fight and another for out of one: with "Only while fighting" off, the time they are shown while you gear up away from any monster does not use up the fight's, so a weapon wielded on the way (a pickaxe in the lobby of the Chambers of Xeric) still gets its pieces and their figure once you attack. Dying, logging in or hopping lets each be shown once more. The wrong attack prayer always stays until it is put right.

And these are fixed, so there is less to set up:

| What | Fixed at |
|---|---|
| A boss, for the attack style wait | combat level 150 and up. Against anything else the style is pointed out at once |
| Better weapon in the inventory | never sooner than 5 ticks (3 seconds) after it became true |
| The monster you are fighting | the last one you attacked, while it is alive, for up to 100 ticks (a minute) without attacking it; once it dies or leaves the scene it is kept on a Slayer task of it for the next one ("After a kill on task", above) and about a second otherwise; forgotten at once when you die, walk more than 20 tiles away, attack a monster that is not in the table, log out or hop |
| The best attack style | pointed out for any gain at all; styles that tie are left alone |

## The attack style

- A small notice in the plugin's overlay: the weapon's icon and the best style ("Jab (crush)"). Red while another style is selected.
- An outline around that style's button while the Combat Options tab is open.
- Works for any weapon and any worn gear: the attack bonuses come from RuneLite's item stats.
- The notice ends with the gain in percent ("Chop (slash) +12%"); "Show the gain %" turns every percentage off.

It compares the styles with the standard accuracy and max hit formulas, using your boosted Attack and Strength levels, your active prayers, your worn bonuses and the monster's defence against stab, slash and crush. Effects that change every style alike (Slayer helmet, Salve amulet, weapons that hit several times) are left out, since they do not change which style is best. The few that depend on the damage type are included: Inquisitor's armour on crush, Osmumten's fang on stab, and the Corporeal Beast.

With a bow, or a staff on autocast, there is no melee style in use and the plugin says nothing.

## The better weapon: damage per second, or time to kill

By default a weapon in your inventory is compared with the one in your hand by the damage per second each deals with its pieces, as everywhere else in this plugin. The setting "Compare weapons by" has a second choice, "Time to kill": then they are compared by how long each takes to kill the monster from its full hitpoints, as the OSRS Wiki DPS calculator works out its "time to kill": from the chances of the damage of one attack (every hitsplat of it summed), the mean number of attacks until the hitpoints are gone, what a hit deals beyond the hitpoints left being wasted, times the ticks between attacks. Against a monster of many hitpoints this is the damage per tick; against one of a few, a fast weapon that kills in a hit or two beats a slow one that deals more per second: on a small Nylocas Toxobolos (8 hitpoints in a trio) a blowpipe kills one about every 1.5 seconds, a Twisted bow every 3.3, though the bow deals a little more per second. With "Time to kill" the notice gives how much sooner the weapon kills ("+117%"), and says "(low HP)" after it when the damage per tick alone would not have pointed at it; with the damage per second it never says "(low HP)".

With "Time to kill", the Nylocas Ischyros (grey) of the Nylocas room's waves are fought with one melee weapon, small and big alike, so a melee weapon is compared on them by the time to kill one of them, the small and the big in the share they come in: 25 small and 16 big in the OSRS Wiki's chart of the 31 waves (Hard Mode has the same waves; Entry Mode's have no chart and are taken in the same share). The same weapon is pointed at whichever size you are fighting. The Toxobolos and the Hagios are compared one by one, as any monster.

The time to kill is not worked out, and the damage per tick decides, for a set whose damage this plugin does not put as chances: enchanted bolts with their effect, Dharok's set, the keris on a kalphite, the limits of Verzik's first phase and of the Hueycoatl's tail, Zulrah's and the Fragment of Seren's caps, the Twinflame staff, Ahrim's set, a monster a hit kills or every attack lands at the max on, and a monster of more than 10,000 hitpoints. Those chances must give the damage per tick itself; when a rule left out would make them differ, they are not used. A weapon that hits several monsters (a Scythe of vitur on small Nylocas side by side, a Venator bow, a barrage) is counted on one, as everywhere in this plugin.

The Dual macuahuitl has a figure: two hits, up to half the max hit each, the second only when the first lands (the calculator's); with the Blood moon set, whose attacks can come a tick sooner, it has none.

## The switch pieces

The plugin looks at the combat class of the weapon in your hand (melee, ranged or magic) and at your inventory. A carried piece is "left behind" when it offers that class more than what you are wearing in its slot (attack bonus plus damage bonus). No monster's stats are involved.

- Their icons are drawn in the plugin's overlay, in inventory order, with "N left".
- Optionally (off by default), each one is also marked in your inventory, like RuneLite's Inventory Tags: an outline of the item, a tint over the item, a box, a circle or a filled box, in the colour, thickness and intensity (5% to 100%) you pick, with an optional pulse.
- A worn piece that does less than an empty slot with the weapon in your hand (melee gloves with a staff: a negative attack bonus and nothing to make up for it), when nothing you carry is better there, is shown in the same row crossed out ("1 off") and circled in the Worn Equipment tab. Can be turned off.
- A piece disappears when you put it on; everything disappears when the switch is complete.
- The weapon and the ammunition are your own choice and are never listed. A shield is not asked for while you hold a two-handed weapon.
- By default this only runs while a monster you attacked is remembered, so gearing up at a bank does not light up.

## Limits

- The best attack style is worked out for melee weapons only; the other reminders cover melee, ranged and magic.
- The monster must be in the table (3,980 NPC ids in the one bundled; see "The monster table"). Every id the OSRS Wiki gives a version of a monster has a row of its own with that version's numbers (Vorkath after the quest, the Lizardman shamans of the caves, the lighthouse's Dagannoths, Dusk's last form...). An id the table does not list (a Deadman Mode boss, a monster newer than the table) is looked up by the monster's name and counted with the figures of the first one of that name. The awakened fights of Desert Treasure II's bosses (the Leviathan, Duke Sucellus, Vardorvis, the Whisperer) have the same NPC ids as the fights after the quest and rows of their own: they are counted once the game's boss health display shows more hitpoints than the fight after the quest has (2700 against 900 on the Leviathan), and as the fight after the quest until it shows them.
- Yama's magic defence is counted as while the one Yama attacks uses magic (+60, the calculator's "Tank using magic"): with a magic weapon in hand you are taken to be holding the boss. In a duo where the other player holds it with melee it is -30, and magic deals more than the figure says; Yama's third phase is counted as the first two.
- Against a vampyre of tier 2 or 3 an ordinary weapon deals nothing, as in the game, and the weapons that hurt one are counted as the calculator counts them: the Hallowed flail a quarter more accurate and a quarter more each hit, the Sunspear a quarter more accurate and a half more each hit, a silver weapon a tenth more each hit on tier 1 and no more than 10 each hit on tier 2 (the calculator's limit; the OSRS Wiki speaks of full damage), and Efaritay's aid a tenth more each hit of those first, 15% more accuracy with a silver weapon, and half of each hit of any other weapon on tier 2. The calculator's code never takes silver bolts for silver (its list names them in a way it does not match): here they are silver, under the same rules. The Blisterwood and Ivandis weapons and the Rod of Ivandis have no figure.
- The Tormented Demon is counted with half of the attacks meeting it without defence (each of those lands) and half rolling against its defence, in every combat class: the plugin does not yet read from the game whether its flames are out, which is when every attack lands. It stays a monster of phases: only a weapon of the class in your hand is ever pointed at.
- Fareed takes melee only with Ice gloves on: the plugin does not look at the gloves.
- Karil's set with the Amulet of the damned is counted without its second hit (a quarter of the attacks, half the damage): the calculator does not count it either.
- No figure for three weapons that attack only somewhere else: the rat bone weapons only rats (the table does not say which monsters are rats), the Dark bow (bh) only in Bounty Hunter's areas and a few minigames, the Drygore blowpipe only in the leagues (OSRS Wiki, Bone mace, Dark bow (bh), Drygore blowpipe).
- One NPC id can be two versions of a monster in the OSRS Wiki's data (Black Eye Bethel with each of her weapons, the Giant goblin's, Yama's third phase, the Doom of Mokhaiotl at each delve, the Eclipse Moon's Clone): the game gives them the same id, and the plugin counts each with the numbers of its row (the first version, or the fight's own: see "The monster table"). The awakened fights of Desert Treasure II's bosses are the exception (above).
- A monster whose defence changes during the fight is compared at its listed values (Vardorvis at full health, awakened or not), except Araxxor, who is counted enraged from a quarter of his hitpoints on (35 more Defence), as the calculator counts him. The Wardens of the Tombs of Amascut's third phase are counted enraged (Defence 180) from the moment the phantom of Amascut behind them opens her mouth, which is when they enrage.
- The switch pieces are chosen by their bonuses. An item you carry on purpose that beats what you wear in its slot will be marked.

## Conditional effects of armour

Some armour gives more than the numbers on the item, under a condition. The plugin knows these (sizes from the OSRS Wiki DPS calculator):

| Armour | Condition | How it counts |
|---|---|---|
| Crystal helm, legs, body (of Prifddinas, charged) | A Crystal bow or Bow of Faerdhinen in hand | Each piece ranks above its plain bonuses (5%, 10%, 15% accuracy and half that in damage) |
| Obsidian helmet, platebody, platelegs | A TzHaar weapon in hand, all three worn | A tenth more accuracy and max hit |
| Berserker necklace | A TzHaar weapon in hand | A fifth more on each hit |
| Ahrim's set and Amulet of the damned | Ahrim's staff in hand, all five worn | A quarter of the spells deal 30% more |
| Void mage helm with the Void set | All four worn | 45% more effective Magic level; 5% more magic damage with the Elite set |
| Virtus mask, top, bottom (the echo Virtus too) | Ancient Magicks cast from a staff | 3% more magic damage each |
| Dizana's quiver (charged), Blessed Dizana's quiver, Dizana's max cape | Arrows, bolts or javelins shot | 10 more ranged accuracy and 1 more ranged strength |
| Seeking arrows (bronze to dragon, broad) | Shot by the bow in hand | Every hit that lands deals at least 3 (OSRS Wiki, Seeking dragon arrow) |
| Tome of fire, water, earth (charged) | A spell of that element on autocast | A tenth more magic damage; it is not asked off for a shield with better numbers |
| Inquisitor's helm, hauberk, plateskirt | A crush style selected | A little more each (0.5% to 1%) |
| Barrows sets (Ahrim's, Dharok's, Guthan's, Karil's, Torag's, Verac's), at any state of wear | The brother's weapon in hand and his helm, body and legs all on you | The three pieces are asked for together and none is asked off for a piece with better numbers; with a piece missing each is compared like any other. A broken piece ("0") is no part of a set |
| Dharok's set | All four pieces worn | The max hit grows with the hitpoints you have lost, as in the game |
| Void Knight set | The helm of the style in use is worn or carried | The whole set of that style is asked for; only the helm changes between styles |

And some pieces are worn for a reason that is not in their bonuses. The plugin never asks you to take these off for a piece with better numbers: Amulet of avarice, Void, Obsidian armour and Berserker necklace, the Barrows sets and Amulet of the damned, the Moons of Peril sets, and the Lightbearer. A Ring of shadows is a teleport and is never asked for.

Melee pieces are compared by the damage type of the style you have selected (stab, slash or crush), not by their best one.

## Slayer task

The plugin reads your Slayer task from the game itself (how many are left and the task's name) and knows, from a bundled table by NPC id, which monsters belong to each task. No other plugin is involved.

- On a monster of your task, a Slayer helmet or Black mask (of any colour) left in the inventory is listed with the other switch pieces, and its bonus is in the figure: melee for any of them, ranged and magic for the imbued ones. It is listed only when the set with it deals more to that monster than what you have on your head now.
- Against the undead a Salve amulet is listed the same way. The two do not stack: with a Salve amulet that works for the style in use, the helmet is not asked for.
- Off task the helmet is compared like any other helmet; so is the amulet against anything that is not undead.
- A boss task is of the boss the game names (the game's boss task id, read the way RuneLite's Slayer plugin reads it), and of the ones the OSRS Wiki lets kill in its place: Artio for Callisto, Spindel for Venenatis, Calvar'ion for Vet'ion, the Deranged archaeologist for the Crazy one (not on Konar's task), the six brothers of the Barrows, the three Dagannoth Kings, Dusk and Dawn, the Abyssal Sire's respiratory systems. The awakened fights of Desert Treasure II's bosses are off task: Slayer equipment does nothing there.
- A task of Krystilia's counts only inside the Wilderness. A task of Konar's counts wherever the monster is: the helmet does nothing outside the place she gave, but the plugin does not know where that place begins and ends.
- When the plugin cannot tell (no monster yet, a task it does not know, a boss task whose boss the game does not name), nothing changes: the helmet is neither asked for nor asked off.

A blessing left in the inventory is listed when the ammunition slot is empty and the weapon in hand does not use it (melee, magic, a blowpipe, a crystal bow, thrown weapons). Ammunition itself is never asked for.

## Defence drains

Everything above is worked out with the Defence the monster has left after special attacks: Dragon warhammer, Elder maul, Bandos godsword, Arclight and Darklight (and the Arclight without charges, which works as the Darklight), Emberlight, Tonalztics of Ralos (charged or not: uncharged it hits once), Accursed sceptre, Seercull, Eye of Ayak, bone dagger, Dorgeshuun crossbow and Barrelchest anchor. Your own are seen as they land, the way RuneLite's Special Attack Counter sees them. Your party's arrive through that plugin's party messages, so it must be on for you and for whoever specs; it sends nothing for the Arclight and the Tonalztics without charges. This is always on: there is no option for it.

Each special attack is taken off the levels the one before left, in the order they landed, as the game does it (OSRS Wiki: "each reduction is calculated from the current temporary level"): a Bandos godsword's hit before a Dragon warhammer leaves more Defence than after it. The order is the one the hits land in: an Elder maul's hit lands 2 ticks after its attack, and the Special Attack Counter sends a party member's maul on the tick of the attack, so a godsword landing in between is counted first. Each one is worked out as the OSRS Wiki DPS calculator works it out (same rounding, the same floors: Verzik and Vardorvis cannot be drained, Nex stops at 250...), a Bandos godsword's damage beyond the Defence left going on to Strength, Attack, Magic and Ranged; the calculator takes them in one order of its own, and its figures are the plugin's when they landed in that order. The game's rules the calculator does not have come from the OSRS Wiki:

- Tekton: a miss still lowers 5%, or 10 levels with the godsword.
- The Accursed sceptre takes Defence and Magic to 85% of the full levels and no lower: on a level already lowered more it does nothing. The Seercull lowers Magic only while it is full.
- The bone dagger and the Dorgeshuun crossbow lower Defence by their damage only while it is full. The Barrelchest anchor lowers Defence by a tenth of its damage (rounded down: the wiki does not say how it rounds), or Attack, Ranged or Magic once Defence is down to 1.
- Sotetseg: every drain is undone each time the team is sent into the maze ("any defence drain will be reset each time players are transported to the shadow realm"): what was counted on him is dropped as he goes into his maze form.
- Kephri: her Defence is back to full in her last phase ("the defence level is reset for the final phase"): what was counted on her shielded forms is dropped as she goes into it.
- The Pestilent Bloat: its stomp, on the 30th tick after it shuts down, restores its Defence ("restoring any drained Defence levels"): what was counted on it before the stomp is dropped then (the tick of the stomp is the wiki's, not measured).
- Floors: the Phantom Muspah stops at 125, the Doom of Mokhaiotl at 60, the Fragment of Seren at 60 and the Menaphite Akh at 100.
- Fights immune to drains take none of them: the awakened fights of Desert Treasure II's bosses (from the moment they are counted as awakened, see "Limits"), Demonic Brutus and the Moons of Peril. Yama with a contract is drained as any other Yama: the wiki's page on drains lists contracted fights as immune, Yama's own page says the drains act on them, and the plugin does not tell a contracted fight from another.
- The Alchemical Hydra: in each of its first three phases, from the start of the phase until the right vent sprays it (its roar, "Roaaaaaaaaaaar!"), its hitsplats are a quarter of the damage, and a Bandos godsword's and a Barrelchest anchor's drain go by the damage before that cut: they are counted from four times the hitsplat (the game's rounding of the quarter is not known: up to 3 levels off).

What was counted on a monster is dropped when it dies, goes away or changes into a form with another Defence, and when the game gives the Defence back on a change of form: the Phantom Muspah entering its shielded phase, Galvek going on to his next phase. Monsters win their lowered stats back with time (OSRS Wiki, Temporary skill drain: "Stats are restored at a rate of 1 per 60 seconds"; "Most monsters follow the same rules as players"): one level of each lowered stat every 100 ticks, counted from the first special attack on the monster, each level back counting for the special attacks after it; the magic defence the Eye of Ayak lowers does not come back (it is not a stat). Faster or slower where the wiki gives the monster a rate of its own: Tekton every 15 ticks, Vasa Nistirio and Vespula 10, the Guardians of the Chambers 8, the Glowing crystal 9, the flying Vespine soldier 16, the Lizardman shamans of the Chambers 20, the Skeletal Mystics 25, the Abyssal portal 45, Phosani's Nightmare 5, the Nightmare 20 levels a minute. The calculator has no time. Vet'ion and Calvar'ion give their Defence back going into their second form.

Not counted: the Fragment of Seren shows a hit above 22 as 22 to 24, and the drain goes by the hit (OSRS Wiki): a godsword hit that shows 22 to 24 is counted as shown. A special attack that lands for 0 is counted as a miss, as the Special Attack Counter counts it. The Doom of Mokhaiotl's Defence comes back on each delve as long as the game brings it back as a new monster (not yet seen in the game).

### The Defence left

While a special attack that lowers Defence is counted on the monster you fight, a line of its own under the other notices gives its Defence before them and the one the figures use now, and how many were counted, misses included: "Def 246 -> 158 (3 specs)". With an estimated one among them the Defence now gets "~" ("Def 246 -> ~158 (3 specs)"). It is not shown for a monster whose Defence cannot be lowered (Verzik Vitur, Vardorvis, the fights immune to drains), and it goes with what was counted: when the monster dies or is forgotten, on a change of form that gives its Defence back, after each of Sotetseg's mazes and the Pestilent Bloat's stomp. The special attacks lower the monster's levels, not its defence bonuses, so the line follows the weapon in your hand: with a melee or ranged weapon it gives the Defence level, which both roll against; with a magic weapon, which rolls against the Magic level, it gives the Magic level when a special attack lowered it (a Bandos godsword's damage beyond the Defence, the Accursed sceptre, the Seercull) and nothing when none did, except on the monsters whose magic defence is rolled with their Defence (the Ice demon, for one), where it gives the Defence. RuneLite's Special Attack Counter shows each weapon's drain from your party; this shows the Defence that results, the specs from outside the party included. "Show the Defence left" turns it off (on by default).

### Dinh's bulwark's special attack

With Dinh's bulwark in hand in its Pummel style and half your special attack energy left, a line gives what its Shield Bash would deal: "Spec: 41.2 (max 96)" on the monster you fight, which it hits twice, and in a multicombat area "Spec 4 targets: 41.2 (max 96), others 20.6 (max 48)": every other monster within 5 tiles that can be attacked takes one hit, up to 10 monsters in all (the mean of the others' figures and the highest of their max hits). Each hit is the Pummel style's attack with 20% more accuracy, rolled against the monster's crush defence, at the bulwark's usual max hit (OSRS Wiki, Dinh's bulwark, "Special attack"; the calculator has no figure for it). "Show Dinh's bulwark's spec" turns it off. With the bulwark in hand no other weapon is pointed at, as with the weapons held for their special attack.

### Special attacks outside the party (estimated)

With "Estimate specs" on (on by default since a ToB with 4 players decided 6 of 6 and the CoX measurement had no wrong decision; one option for the Elder maul, the Dragon warhammer and the Bandos godsword), the Elder maul and Dragon warhammer special attacks of the other players on the monster you fight are counted too when their specs do not come through the party: players outside your party, or in it without the Special Attack Counter. The client shows no message for them, so they are estimated from what it shows, and counted only when there is no doubt:

- the player's target is that very monster (not another of the same kind) and the player starts the weapon's special attack animation with that weapon seen in hand;
- on the tick the hit lands (2 ticks after the animation for the Elder maul, 1 for the Dragon warhammer, measured in a Chambers of Xeric raid against the party's Special Attack Counter), every hitsplat on the monster that is not yours is above 0: it hit (35% or 30% of the Defence left, as above); or every one is 0: it missed, which lowers nothing except Tekton's 5%.

Not counted: a tick with some hitsplats at 0 and some above (in a big raid several players hit on the same tick and a hitsplat does not say whose it is; in the raid measured, those undecided ticks held all the misses of the Elder maul), a tick with no hitsplat, and every other special attack (the Bandos godsword, below, and the rest drain by their damage, which a shared tick does not tell). That leaves the Defence a little higher than it is rather than ever lower. Nothing is estimated with the game's hitsplat tinting turned off (not measured), for your own special attacks, or for a player whose special attacks have come through the party once: theirs are counted exactly, and an estimate made before their message comes is replaced by it, so no one is counted twice. A player who leaves the party (or a change of party) is estimated again. An estimate takes its place among the other drains by the tick it landed.

The Bandos godsword special attacks of the same players are counted too (not measured yet with a small team: in the Chambers of Xeric raids measured every Bandos godsword special attack landed on a tick with 2 to 20 hitsplats, where it is not counted), only when there is no doubt whose hitsplat is the godsword's: its special attack animation (7642, or 7643 for the ornament) with the godsword seen in hand, the player aiming at that very monster, and on the tick the hit lands (1 tick after the animation, measured) exactly one hitsplat on the monster that is not yours. That hitsplat's damage is the drain, as above: Defence first, what is left over on to Strength, Attack, Magic and Ranged. A 0 is a miss and lowers nothing, except on Tekton, where it drains 10 (OSRS Wiki). Two hitsplats or more, or none, and it is not counted. The party's message replaces it as for the others.

While the Defence used counts an estimated special attack, the figures on screen get "~" ("~+12%") and the first notice says "spec estimated" (in the language chosen).

## Chambers of Xeric

Inside the raid, with "Chambers of Xeric scaling" on (the default), the raid's monsters are counted with the levels and hitpoints the raid gives them, as the OSRS Wiki DPS calculator scales them: the party size the raid was scaled for, Challenge Mode and the party's highest combat level, all read from the game. Challenge Mode is read from its varbit, which reads 0 for a member who is not the party's leader (14 Challenge Mode raids measured), and from the game's own message on the tick the raid begins, "Map Layout: Challenge Mode (Full).", which every one of them had; it is kept for each account until the next raid begins, so a relog inside the raid keeps it. A big party raises the Skeletal Mystics' Magic level, for instance, and with it what the Twisted bow deals to them; Tekton's Defence grows less than the others' in Challenge Mode, and the Glowing crystal's not at all. The game does not give the party's highest Hitpoints level, so it is taken as 99, and the Guardians' hitpoints are worked out with 99 Mining for each member. The special attacks counted on a monster (see "Defence drains") are taken off the scaled levels, in the order they landed. Outside the raid, or turned off, the table's levels are used as they are.

## Theatre of Blood

Inside the raid, with "Theatre of Blood scaling" on (the default), its monsters are counted with the hitpoints the party gives them, as the OSRS Wiki DPS calculator scales them: in normal and Hard Mode a party of three or fewer has 75% of them and four 87.5%; Entry Mode has its own table. The party is read from the game: the places of the party that hold someone, the most since the raid began (a player leaving does not lower it). Only the hitpoints scale in the Theatre (they count for ruby bolts); Hard Mode and Entry Mode monsters have levels of their own, found by the id the game gives each boss in combat (Sotetseg in a normal raid is not the Entry Mode one; Verzik's transitions count as the phase the wiki lists them in; the Hard Mode wave Nylocas as the normal ones, as the wiki lists them). Verzik's first phase is counted as the calculator counts it: each hit up to 10 for melee and 3 for anything else. On Xarpus and Verzik's second phase, in every mode, the Scythe of vitur (Holy and Sanguine too) is counted at one attack every 16/3 ticks (about 5.33) instead of 5, so 15/16 of the calculator's figure: the melee has to step back from Xarpus's poison and Verzik's bombs, and a 5-tick weapon loses one tick of its cycle (OSRS Wiki, Theatre of Blood/Strategies: "This uses 15/16 available ticks to damage the boss, or effectively a 5.33 tick attack speed"). The other 5-tick weapons keep their speed. On Xarpus this changes the comparison of melee weapons; on Verzik's second phase it is also what Tumeken's shadow and the other carried weapons are compared against. Outside the raid, turned off, or with no party read, the table's hitpoints are used as they are.

## Tombs of Amascut

Inside the raid, with "Tombs of Amascut scaling" on (the default), its monsters are counted with the raid level, the path levels and the party size read from the game, as the calculator does it: the monster's defence roll grows with the raid level ((250 + raid level) / 250 of it, after the defence drains; not for Kephri's scarab overlords), and its hitpoints with the raid level, the path level and the party (the party the raid began with: a player leaving does not lower it) (they count for ruby bolts and for the Keris partisan of the sun). The raid's own rules count either way: Tumeken's shadow multiplies its bonuses by four instead of three, Osmumten's fang rolls its accuracy twice the raid's way, and the Keris partisan of the sun is a quarter more accurate below a quarter of the monster's hitpoints. On the Wardens of the second phase every attack lands, and ranged and magic deal 15% to 60% of the max hit by how much the attack roll beats a third of the Warden's defence roll (the Twisted bow's accuracy counts twice there); every melee hit on a Warden's core is a max hit, the core counted as the calculator counts it (the Warden whose core is out). The other monsters inside the Tombs (the baboons of Apmeken, the crocodile of Crondis, the scarab of Scabaras, Kephri's swarms, agile scarabs and eggs) are counted inside the raid too, though the calculator counts them as outside: Tumeken's shadow multiplies its bonuses by four ("four within the Tombs of Amascut", OSRS Wiki), the Keris partisan of amascut has its bonuses, Osmumten's fang and the Keris partisan of the sun follow the raid's rules, and the raid level raises their defence roll ("all enemies within the raid"), but not Kephri's scarabs' ("no longer scale with Raid Level"). Their hitpoints are not scaled: the baboons' "are now static", and the others' are not given. The Wardens of the third phase are counted enraged (Defence 180, and the defence drains counted before are dropped) once the phantom of Amascut behind them opens her mouth.

## The Fractured Archive

The calculator does not have the raid yet, so its bosses' rows hold only what is confirmed: the OSRS Wiki's infoboxes of 21 October 2026, in-game examines and recorded fights. The Archive Guardian in flight (16659) and on the ground (16660), Ezekiel (16652), Bork (16640) and the Smorks (16641), Moia (16667), Dreadnoth the Jellied (16665), Xarth (16684) and Ocellus in his three phases (16673 and 16674, 16675, 16677). The Dagon'hai have no numbers and no row.

- Only the hitpoints scale with the party (measured; the Defence and the other levels do not). They are taken from the boss health bar, which shows them scaled, while it shows the monster fought; until it does, from the OSRS Wiki's table for the size of the team (each boss's "Hitpoints", 26 October 2026: the Archive Guardian in flight and on the ground, Bork, the Smorks, Ezekiel, Dreadnoth, Xarth and Ocellus's first phase and the rest; a solo raid as a duo), the team being the raid's party places held, the most since it began. Moia, the Dagon'hai and The Blade have no table: there ruby bolts and the time to kill have no figure until the bar shows them.
- The Defence reduction caps of the official hotfix of 20 October 2026 ("The Fractured Archive - Out Today"): the Archive Guardian, Bork, Ezekiel, Moia, Xarth and Ocellus keep 60% of their base Defence whatever lands (156, 180, 84, 117, 102, 129 and 150); Dreadnoth, the Smorks, the Dagon'hai and The Blade can be taken to 0. Ocellus keeps his drains from one quadrant of his third phase to the next. Each hit of the Tonalztics of Ralos that lands is a drain. Nex's crush defence is +35 (the same update; the calculator's data still has +60).
- The Archive Guardian has the opening's Defence 260 and Magic 250 in both forms, so the special attacks counted in flight go on when it lands and rises (measured: its Defence does not come back). Its Defence falls and its Magic rises later in the fight for reasons not known: not counted. No Ancient Magicks hurt it (OSRS Wiki); in flight only a halberd's melee reaches it.
- Ocellus: the wiki says the Defence reduction "works on all phases", not how. After a special attack in one phase, the next shows "Def ?", and the figures count only the special attacks of the new phase.
- Ezekiel: no drain is confirmed on him (an examine after two Dragon warhammers did not match): the figures keep his Defence 140 and the line says "Def ?" once a special attack is counted.
- Bork: flat armour 8 and magic resistance 0 with the Smorks at full health, 2 more of each every 25% of the Smorks' health lost, up to 14 and 6 (OSRS Wiki, Bork; the magic resistance comes off every magic hit that lands). Stunned by the Smorks' heavy cannonball (his "Oof." or the stun's animation, until "Bork very angry!!" or 14 ticks), his flat armour is 0 (wiki) and the line says "Def ?": his Defence in the stun was never examined.
- Dreadnoth is counted as a demon 25% more vulnerable to demonbane (the examine). His Jellies (16666) have a row with no numbers: they are not the Slayer Jelly, no weapon is pointed at on them, and the line says "Def ?".

## The best weapon in the bank

Outside the raids (not in the Chambers of Xeric, the Theatre of Blood, the Tombs of Amascut or the Fractured Archive, nor on any of their monsters), "Bank: <weapon> +X%" names the weapon of the bank last seen open that would deal the most to the monster fought in place of the one in hand, with everything else worn as it is (a weapon of two hands without the shield), counted as the weapon notice counts a carried weapon. Only a weapon, never armour or a shield; not one without its charges, nor a bow or crossbow without ammunition it fires on; another dress of a weapon is the weapon itself. It is worked out again only when the monster, what is worn or the bank changes. "Better weapon in the bank" turns it off, and "Bank weapon: minimum gain" (10% by default) sets how much more it must deal.

## Items with a rule of their own

The Confliction gauntlets: with a magic weapon of one hand (the Eye of Ayak, the Sanguinesti staff, the Kodai wand...), a cast rolls its accuracy as the calculator does for them, a second roll that can save a miss; with Tumeken's shadow, which takes both hands, only their bonuses count. Against a monster your magic already lands on almost every time (a Nylocas Hagios) they change little; against a high magic defence much more (the Eye of Ayak on Vorkath: about a fifth more damage from the roll alone).

Items in another dress count as the item: an ornament kit (the Holy and Sanguine Scythe of vitur, the Radiant oathplate, the Sanguine torva, the Twisted ancestral, "(or)"), a locked one ("(l)"), a trim, a recolour (the slayer helmets, the Bow of Faerdhinen and the Blade of Saeldor (c), the crystal armour), a corrupted one of Bounty Hunter and the same item under other ids. Their list (`variants.tsv`, `tools/gen_variants.py`, about 1,800 items) joins the wiki DPS calculator's own list of items "identical in function" with RuneLite's item names and stats: a dress has the item's very bonuses. Imbued "(i)", enchanted "(e)" and the Avernic treads with other boots in them are other items, with their own bonuses or effect.

As the calculator counts them: the Nightmare's and Phosani's totems take twice the damage of magic (every id of the four totems); the Hueycoatl's tail limits each hit to 4, or 9 with a crush style while crush is your highest attack bonus (then a miss deals 1) or an earth spell; the Corporeal Beast halves each hit of ranged too, not only of melee without a corpbane stab, and a ruby bolt's effect is whole; every magic attack lands at its max hit on the Royal Titans' elementals, and so do the classes the calculator lists for a few other monsters (the Tombs of Amascut's baboons, the Judge of Yama); Araxxor's Acidic and Mirrorback araxytes take the max hit of every attack of the Noxious halberd, of a crush style while crush is your highest attack bonus and of a crossbow while ranged is, and so does the Ruptura araxyte (the OSRS Wiki has the same sentence on the three pages; the calculator has the rule for the two others only, and the wiki decides the mechanics); every attack lands on the Spawn of the Abyssal Sire; Scurrius's Giant rats and the Flowers (Hespori's, A Night at the Theatre's) die in one hit, so the faster weapon is the better one there; on the Abyssal Sire's Respiratory systems every hit that lands is at least half the max hit, and a demonbane weapon's hit that lands (the Scorching bow's) kills one. ## The monster table

The monsters' defences and the weapons' categories come bundled with the plugin, and that is what it starts with. With "Update the monster table" on (the default), the plugin asks its own GitHub repository once at startup whether a newer copy of the same three tables has been published, and uses it: a monster the game added, or one whose defence it changed, is known without waiting for a new version of the plugin. Each file is checked against the SHA-256 the list gives for it, must read as a table and must not be much smaller than the bundled one; anything off, and the bundled table stays. Only numbers and names are read; nothing is asked for during a fight, and nothing of yours is sent. Turned off, the plugin makes no request at all.

## Data

Weapon categories, their styles and the monsters' defence come from the [OSRS Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki) through its DPS calculator project; each monster's Slayer category (`tools/gen_slayer.py`) and the NPC ids of each version of a monster (`tools/gen_ids.py`) come from the wiki's monster infoboxes. `tools/gen_data.py` rebuilds them; the rules the wiki decides against the calculator are written in it, and a test fails if the table loses one. The tests compare the recommended style with the calculator's over 2,350 weapon and monster pairs. `tools/gen_sweep.py` runs every monster of the calculator's data that has a row in the table against sets of gear that cover the weapons' rules, and every weapon of the table against a few monsters; each figure the plugin shows is within 0.1% of the calculator's (`SweepTest`), and every case where the two differ by more is one of the rules above where the OSRS Wiki decides.

## Bug or suggestion

Click "Bug or suggestion" in the plugin's settings: it opens a window where you write what is wrong or what you would like. Under your words it shows exactly what goes with them: the plugin's name, the monster you were fighting, the ids of the items you wear and carry, and the map region. No name of yours or of your account.

- "Send the report" sends it to the author's Google Form. Nothing is sent until you press it.
- "Open the report on GitHub" opens a new issue with the text filled in, for you to post.
- "Copy the report" puts it on the clipboard.
